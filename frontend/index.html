Why EKF State-Space > Static ML

Static Ridge/Random Forests only inspect instantaneous rows. Our Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) tracks hidden gut carbs and adapts each patient's Insulin Sensitivity ($S_I$) online with 90% confidence intervals.

What MAGE Measures

Mean Amplitude of Glycemic Excursions (MAGE) quantifies sharp post-meal spikes and crashes (>1 SD), capturing metabolic instability better than average glucose alone.

Clarke Error Grid (Zones A–E)

The clinical benchmark for glucose prediction safety: Zone A (within 20% of true CGM) and Zone B (benign error) are clinically safe; Zones C–E represent risky errors.

What This Digital Twin CAN Claim

  • Assimilates real longitudinal wearable + CGM streams (5-min CGM, HR, HRV, EDA, ACC) via an Extended Kalman Filter.
  • Learns individualized latent Insulin Sensitivity ($S_I$) online and outputs calibrated 90% confidence intervals.
  • Outperforms static tabular regression on 30-min ahead forecasting with 100% Clarke Zone A+B clinical safety.
  • Simulates counterfactual lifestyle interventions dynamically via coupled physiological ODEs.

What This Digital Twin CANNOT Claim

  • Not a clinical diagnostic tool or medical device; never prescribes medication or insulin doses.
  • What-If trajectories are physiology-informed ODE simulations, not randomized clinical trial outcomes.
  • Long-term hard endpoints (10-year MI/stroke survival) require multi-year EHR follow-up beyond 10-day wearables.
